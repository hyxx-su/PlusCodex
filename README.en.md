# PlusCodex

> Check your Codex usage faster and more easily.

PlusCodex is **a macOS menu bar utility that shows your remaining Codex usage and reset times at a glance**.

Instead of checking your usage manually each time,
see what you have left right from the menu bar and receive notifications when they matter.
